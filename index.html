📖 How to Use

  1. To Hide: Upload a normal image, select the file/text you want to hide, enter a passcode, and download the Encrypted Image.
  2. To Extract: Upload the Encrypted Image, enter the same passcode, and download your hidden file.

⚠️ Important Notes

  • PNG Format Only: Always use PNG images. JPG/JPEG formats compress data and will destroy your hidden files.
  • WhatsApp Warning: Do NOT send Stego-Images via normal WhatsApp or Instagram messages. It will compress and corrupt the data. Always send as a Document, or use Google Drive/Telegram.
  • Size Limits: The hidden file cannot be larger than the carrier image's pixel capacity (Usually max 1MB for a standard HD image).
